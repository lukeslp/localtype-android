Modifications by Luke Steuber, 2026: take the rightmost label of ICANN rules,
normalize IDNs to ASCII labels, lowercase, deduplicate and sort. Exclude eleven
labels not recognized by LocalType 0.5.22; these are explicit compatibility
exclusions, not a claim that those suffixes are invalid. Private-section rules
are not used. The resulting 1,437 entries match the earlier recognition set.
The derived file remains under MPL-2.0. No upstream author endorses LocalType.

The complete pinned source, exclusions and build script accompany the prepared
source snapshot under distribution/source/tlds/ and scripts/build-tlds.py.
Run python3 scripts/build-tlds.py --check to verify the generated file. Source
form is also available at the pinned public URL above and is offered under MPL-2.0.

Earlier versions used an IANA root-zone list under a factual-data rationale.
This replacement does not assert or invent a new license for the old IANA file.
