Trained by Luke Steuber on FUTO swipe.futo.org, run swipe-1, training split.
Model: swipe-a2-h96-r1, best checkpoint after eight training epochs.
The recordings are not included. Model export: 1,145,224 bytes.
SHA-256: 665f2879a6294dddfa4487e25ac4504eeea21934645a542addd0f134e6b563e0
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/futo-org/swipe.futo.org
David Lee Miller and Aleksandras Kostarevas, FUTO Swipe: Layout-Agnostic Neural Swipe Decoding, 2026.

The LTSW file contains numeric weights only, not recordings or a word list.
At runtime the decoder reads the separate words_en.txt and words_en_modern.txt
lists, which retain their own CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. Their terms do not establish
that a vocabulary is embedded in the weight export. The upstream authors have
not endorsed LocalType.
