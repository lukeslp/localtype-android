LocalType Portuguese corpus prior (corpus_prior_pt.bin)

corpus_prior_pt.bin holds Portuguese word-pair statistics that LocalType's model/language_build.py
computed with model/prior.py from the two corpora below. The text corpora offered for download by
the Leipzig Corpora Collection are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution licence
(CC BY), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (terms of usage read 2026-10-17 and
2026-10-18 at https://wortschatz-leipzig.de/en/usage).

The corpora were changed to make this file. The text was split into words, one sentence in ten was
held out, and pairs of adjacent words were counted in the rest. The counts were smoothed with
interpolated modified Kneser-Ney and limited to words in the keyboard's Portuguese word list. Only
the 10,000 most frequent previous words were kept, with up to 16 followers each, and the result was
quantized to 8 bits. The file holds no sentence from either corpus.

1. Leipzig Corpora Collection, Portuguese news 2024, 1 million sentences (por_news_2024_1M).
2. Leipzig Corpora Collection, Brazilian Portuguese newscrawl 2011, 1 million sentences
   (por-br_newscrawl_2011_1M).
   https://wortschatz-leipzig.de/en/download
   D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart and U. Quasthoff, "Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig
   Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages", Proceedings of the 8th International Language
   Resources and Evaluation Conference (LREC 2012), 2012.
   Licence: CC BY.

The corpora are provided by their authors as-is and without warranties. The Leipzig Corpora
Collection has not endorsed LocalType. The words kept are the ones also in the keyboard's Portuguese
word list, words_pt.txt (derived from hermitdave/FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0);
only membership in that list was used.
