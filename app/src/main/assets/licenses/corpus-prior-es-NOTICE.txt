LocalType Spanish corpus prior (corpus_prior_es.bin)

corpus_prior_es.bin holds Spanish word-pair statistics that LocalType's model/language_build.py
computed with model/prior.py from the two corpora below. The text corpora offered for download by
the Leipzig Corpora Collection are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution licence
(CC BY), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (terms of usage read 2026-10-17 and
2026-10-18 at https://wortschatz-leipzig.de/en/usage).

The corpora were changed to make this file. The marks ¿ and ¡ were deleted, the text was split into
words, one sentence in ten was held out, and pairs of adjacent words were counted in the rest. The
counts were smoothed with interpolated modified Kneser-Ney and limited to words in the keyboard's
Spanish word list. Only the 10,000 most frequent previous words were kept, with up to 16 followers
each, and the result was quantized to 8 bits. The file holds no sentence from either corpus.

1. Leipzig Corpora Collection, Spanish web 2016, 1 million sentences (spa_web_2016_1M).
2. Leipzig Corpora Collection, Spanish news 2024, 1 million sentences (spa_news_2024_1M).
   https://wortschatz-leipzig.de/en/download
   D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart and U. Quasthoff, "Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig
   Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages", Proceedings of the 8th International Language
   Resources and Evaluation Conference (LREC 2012), 2012.
   Licence: CC BY.

The corpora are provided by their authors as-is and without warranties. The Leipzig Corpora
Collection has not endorsed LocalType. The words kept are the ones also in the keyboard's Spanish
word list, words_es.txt (derived from hermitdave/FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0);
only membership in that list was used.
