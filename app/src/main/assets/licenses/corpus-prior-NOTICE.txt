LocalType corpus prior (corpus_prior.bin)

corpus_prior.bin holds word-pair statistics that LocalType's model/prior.py computed from the three
datasets below. Each is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The datasets were changed to make this file. Their text was split into words, and pairs of adjacent
words were counted. Lines from the two human-written sources (NUS SMS and Taskmaster-1) counted four
times. The counts were smoothed with interpolated modified Kneser-Ney and limited to words in the
keyboard's word lists. Only the 10,000 most frequent previous words were kept, with up to 16
followers each, and the result was quantized to 8 bits. The file holds no sentence or message from
any dataset.

1. NUS SMS Corpus, English portion, release 2015.03.09.
   Tao Chen and Min-Yen Kan, "Creating a Live, Public Short Message Service Corpus: The NUS SMS
   Corpus", Language Resources and Evaluation 47(2), 2013.
   https://doi.org/10.25540/WVM0-4RNX
   Licence: CC BY 4.0.

2. Taskmaster-1, written self-dialogs (USER turns only), TM-1-2019/self-dialogs.json at commit
   d92cb6af3005f1dc09c39e75e7daf4a04905e00b.
   Byrne et al., "Taskmaster-1: Toward a Realistic and Diverse Dialog Dataset", EMNLP 2019.
   Copyright Google LLC.
   https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Taskmaster
   Licence: CC BY 4.0.

3. SODA (allenai/soda), Hugging Face dataset revision fdc848ab0183208ea7808206c91c724414d0a071.
   Kim et al., "SODA: Million-scale Dialogue Distillation with Social Commonsense
   Contextualization", arXiv:2212.10465. Its dialogues were generated by a language model.
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/soda
   Licence: CC BY 4.0.

The datasets are provided by their authors as-is and without warranties; the licence above has the
full disclaimer. Their authors have not endorsed LocalType.

The words kept are the ones also in the keyboard's dictionary: words_en.txt (derived from
hermitdave/FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0) and words_en_modern.txt (written for
LocalType). Only membership in those lists was used. No counts in this file come from them.
