LocalType experimental next-word model (cifg_int8.bin)

Luke Steuber trained this model on the corpus-v3 training split from the five sources below.
The source texts were filtered, normalized, deduplicated and divided into training, development
and test splits. The English vocabulary and a CIFG-LSTM were learned from the training split;
the model was trained for three epochs, selected on human-source development perplexity, and
its weights were quantized to int8. NUS SMS, Taskmaster-1 and OpenAssistant prompter turns were
oversampled eight times; Taskmaster-3 and SODA counted once. This file contains model weights
and a vocabulary, rather than a copy of the source corpus. The source authors have not endorsed
LocalType. The sources are provided as-is and without warranties under their respective licenses.

1. NUS SMS Corpus, English portion, release 2015.03.09.
   Tao Chen and Min-Yen Kan, "Creating a Live, Public Short Message Service Corpus: The NUS SMS
   Corpus", Language Resources and Evaluation 47(2), 2013.
   https://doi.org/10.25540/WVM0-4RNX
   Release mirror: kite1988/nus-sms-corpus, commit bf0a365170d324e61e4025edf363d400f1edd1a6.
   Only English messages were used, with the release's redaction tags removed. Human-written.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

2. Taskmaster-1 written self-dialogs, TM-1-2019/self-dialogs.json.
   Byrne et al., "Taskmaster-1: Toward a Realistic and Diverse Dialog Dataset", EMNLP 2019.
   Copyright Google LLC.
   https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Taskmaster
   Commit d92cb6af3005f1dc09c39e75e7daf4a04905e00b.
   Only USER utterances were used; spoken woz-dialogs were excluded. Human-written.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

3. OpenAssistant Conversations 2 (OpenAssistant/oasst2).
   Köpf et al., "OpenAssistant Conversations: Democratizing Large Language Model Alignment",
   NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks, arXiv:2304.07327.
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenAssistant/oasst2
   Revision 179dd21fc55192153d94adb0e0ce8f69e222bf75, file
   2023-11-05_oasst2_ready.messages.jsonl.gz.
   Only English prompter turns that were not synthetic, deleted or rejected in review were used;
   assistant turns were excluded. Human-written.
   License: Apache License 2.0. A copy is included in Apache-2.0.txt alongside this notice.
   https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

4. Taskmaster-3 (TicketTalk) self-dialogs, TM-3-2020/data/data_00.json through data_19.json.
   Byrne et al., "TicketTalk: Toward human-level performance with end-to-end, transaction-based
   dialog systems", ACL 2021. Copyright Google LLC.
   https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Taskmaster/tree/master/TM-3-2020
   Commit d92cb6af3005f1dc09c39e75e7daf4a04905e00b.
   Only customer utterances (speaker user) were used; agent turns were excluded. Human-written.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

5. SODA (allenai/soda).
   Kim et al., "SODA: Million-scale Dialogue Distillation with Social Commonsense
   Contextualization", arXiv:2212.10465.
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/soda
   Revision fdc848ab0183208ea7808206c91c724414d0a071.
   Both speakers' dialogue turns were used. These dialogues were generated by GPT-3.5
   (InstructGPT); they are synthetic and are not counted as human-written.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The vocabulary's emoji tokens come from LocalType's emoji_en.txt, a word-to-emoji list
written for LocalType. The separate emoji panel uses Unicode and CLDR data; its attribution
