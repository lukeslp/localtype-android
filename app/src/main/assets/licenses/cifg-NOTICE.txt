LocalType experimental next-word model (cifg_int8.bin)

Luke Steuber trained this model on the corpus-v3 training split from the five sources below,
plus the Tatoeba English sentences in item 6. The corpus-v3 texts were filtered, normalized,
deduplicated and divided into training, development and test splits; the English vocabulary was
learned from that training split. The Tatoeba sentences went through the same line rules and an
exact dedupe against corpus-v3 and the scored evaluation sets, and lines naming Tatoeba's stock
characters were dropped. A CIFG-LSTM was then trained for 102,195 updates (about 2.6 passes),
selected on human-source development perplexity, and its weights were quantized to int8. NUS SMS,
Taskmaster-1 and OpenAssistant prompter turns were oversampled eight times; Taskmaster-3, SODA and
Tatoeba counted once. This file contains model weights and a vocabulary, rather than a copy of
the source corpus. The source authors have not endorsed LocalType. The sources are provided as-is
and without warranties under their respective licenses.

   Only English messages were used, with the release's redaction tags removed. Human-written.

   Commit d92cb6af3005f1dc09c39e75e7daf4a04905e00b.
   Only USER utterances were used; spoken woz-dialogs were excluded. Human-written.
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

3. OpenAssistant Conversations 2 (OpenAssistant/oasst2).
