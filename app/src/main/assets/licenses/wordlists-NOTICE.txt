LocalType word lists

words_en.txt and words_en_profanity.txt derive from Hermit Dave's FrequencyWords,
OpenSubtitles 2018 English frequency data:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
The upstream README licenses content under Creative Commons Attribution-
ShareAlike 4.0 International and code separately under MIT. The content terms
apply here. LocalType filters fragments, misspellings and slurs and adds common
contractions. The original acquisition commit was not recorded; the exact
derived bytes are pinned in the source asset inventory. No upstream author
endorses LocalType.

words_en_modern.txt and emoji_en.txt, including the later everyday-reaction
aliases, are original LocalType lists by Luke Steuber, licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. The emoji
aliases are editorial choices, not measured usage counts. The prior public
copies are in https://github.com/lukeslp/chime-pipeline; that publication did
not contain the later aliases.

words_es.txt, words_es_profanity.txt and words_known_es.txt (Spanish) and
words_pt.txt, words_pt_profanity.txt and words_known_pt.txt (Brazilian
Portuguese) derive from the same FrequencyWords project, OpenSubtitles 2018
frequency data in content/2018/es and content/2018/pt_br at commit
525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6, under the same Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International content terms. LocalType kept the
most frequent words, filtered slurs, moved swear words to the profanity lists
and kept words seen 20 or more times as the recognition lists. No upstream
author endorses LocalType.

words_he.txt, words_he_profanity.txt and words_known_he.txt (Hebrew) derive
from the same FrequencyWords project, OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency data in
content/2018/he at commit 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6, under the
same Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International content terms.
LocalType kept the 20,000 most frequent words of Hebrew letters, filtered
slurs, moved swear words to the profanity list and kept words seen 20 or more
times as the recognition list. No upstream author endorses LocalType.

words_cy*.txt (Welsh), words_ga*.txt (Irish) and words_mi*.txt (Maori: words_mi.txt,
words_mi_profanity.txt, words_known_mi.txt) are word counts from each
language's Wikipedia pages-articles dump of 2026-11-01 (cywiki-20261101,
gawiki-20261101, miwiki-20261101; https://dumps.wikimedia.org/). Wikipedia
text is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International by its contributors, and these derived lists keep those terms.
LocalType stripped wiki markup, counted words of each language's own letters,
filtered slurs and moved swear words to the profanity lists. No Wikipedia
contributor or the Wikimedia Foundation endorses LocalType.

words_haw.txt, words_haw_profanity.txt and words_known_haw.txt (Hawaiian) derive
from data/freqlist_haw.txt in dohliam/hawaiian-corpus
(https://github.com/dohliam/hawaiian-corpus), word counts from modern,
non-scriptural Hawaiian text in Ulukau, the Hawaiian Electronic Library. The
upstream data is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0; LocalType's
derived lists are offered under the same CC0 1.0 dedication
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No upstream author
endorses LocalType.

spellings_en_gb.txt is a filtered list of American-to-British spelling pairs
from SCOWL's VarCon 2020.12.07. Its copyright and permission notice is
preserved in VarCon-Copyright.txt, which also marks LocalType's modification.

words_known.txt is a lowercased, deduplicated recognition list derived from
SCOWL 2020.12.07 size 70 (American, British, Canadian and Australian variants
and names), with LocalType's modern-word supplement. SCOWL-Copyright.txt is
preserved verbatim and covers the SCOWL material; the LocalType supplement
retains CC BY-SA 4.0. No SCOWL copyright or permission notice is replaced.
The historical build recipe for the exact SCOWL subset was not retained;
this attribution is not a claim to have independently rebuilt that subset.
Source: https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist/tree/rel-2020.12.07

current_words.txt is an original editorial spelling/score list by Luke Steuber,
licensed under Apache-2.0. It holds names, spellings and manually chosen scores,
not copied articles, messages or measured personal usage. Its public references
are provenance references, not a claim to license those websites' contents.
See Apache-2.0.txt. The source-review snapshot changes only private provenance
comments; every vocabulary row remains unchanged.
