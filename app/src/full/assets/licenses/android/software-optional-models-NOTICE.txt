Software added for the optional models

proofreading. That adds the libraries below to the ones in software-NOTICE. Under
each name are the Maven groups the build takes it from.

libraries, contain third-party components with notices of their own. Those
notices are in the libraries as Google publishes them and are not yet reproduced
on this screen.
