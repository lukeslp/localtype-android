<p>LocalType is an Android keyboard with word predictions and corrections that run on your device. Chime, its small English prediction model, is included. Typing needs no account or model download.</p>

<ul>
<li>Correct clear typos and undo a correction when it is wrong.</li>
<li>Choose word suggestions, save useful text and manage learned word pairs.</li>
<li>Reach accents, IPA symbols, emoji and optional common symbols by holding keys or opening their panels.</li>
<li>Choose British spelling and currency symbols separately.</li>
<li>Adjust the space bar, suggestion bar, one-handed mode, number row and cursor sliding.</li>
</ul>

<p>English is the main language. Spanish, Portuguese, Galician, Hebrew, Arabic, Persian, Welsh, Irish, Scottish Gaelic, Māori, Hawaiian and swipe typing are early experiments and start off. Navajo is a very early experiment that corrects nothing automatically. A dedicated IPA layout is also available. These features still need wider testing.</p>

<p>The F-Droid distribution has no Internet permission. It keeps Chime and the local typing tools, and leaves updates to your installing client. The separate full distribution's optional Gemini Nano and Gemma features are not included.</p>

<p>Typing logs are optional and start off. Password fields do not teach predictions. You can inspect or clear learned words and pairs. Voice input uses Android's on-device recognizer when available, or can switch to another enabled voice keyboard; that other keyboard has its own privacy behavior. The microphone permission is requested the first time you use voice input.</p>

<p>The Contacts tool can insert a name, number or address once you allow it. Contacts stay on the phone, in memory only, and are never learned or sent.</p>
