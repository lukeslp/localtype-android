<p>LocalType is an Android keyboard with word predictions and corrections that run on your device. Chime, its small English prediction model, is included. Typing needs no account or model download.</p>

<p>LocalType is beta software in active development. It changes often, and some features are still early.</p>

<ul>
<li>Correct clear typos and undo a correction when it is wrong.</li>
<li>Choose word suggestions, save useful text and manage learned word pairs.</li>
<li>Give saved text a shortcut word or phrase, and it is offered when you type it.</li>
<li>Add your own words one at a time or import a word list, and block words you never want suggested.</li>
<li>Switch on a word pack for drug names and medical terms or for US politics; both start off.</li>
<li>Use LocalType as the phone's spell checker in other apps, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.</li>
<li>Open a row of tools above the keys: the clipboard, emoji, voice input, undo, an arrow pad, named symbols, text effects and more.</li>
<li>Reach accents, IPA symbols, emoji and optional common symbols by holding keys or opening their panels.</li>
<li>Choose British spelling and currency symbols separately.</li>
<li>Adjust the space bar, suggestion bar, one-handed mode, number row and cursor sliding.</li>
<li>Turn on touch and timing options for a tremor or limited reach. Every key and its held choices are labeled for TalkBack.</li>
<li>Move your settings and words to another phone in one file you save and read yourself.</li>
</ul>

<p>English is the main language. Spanish, Portuguese, Galician, Hebrew, Arabic, Persian, Welsh, Irish, Scottish Gaelic, Māori, Hawaiian and swipe typing are early experiments and start off. Navajo is a very early experiment that corrects nothing automatically. A dedicated IPA layout is also available. Settings and setup are translated into Spanish and Portuguese; the translation is machine-made and not yet checked by a native speaker. These features still need wider testing.</p>

<p>This F-Droid build is a separate app from the direct download on dr.eamer.dev: both can be installed, and neither updates the other. It includes Chime's next-word prediction. It leaves out Proofread, the rewrite tools (Shorter, More formal and Friendlier) and the extra suggestions from a larger on-device model (Gemini Nano or Gemma). It has no Internet permission, and its updates come through F-Droid.</p>

<p>Typing logs are optional and start off. Password fields do not teach predictions. You can inspect or clear learned words and pairs. Voice input uses Android's on-device recognizer when available, or can switch to another enabled voice keyboard; that other keyboard has its own privacy behavior. The microphone permission is requested the first time you use voice input.</p>

<p>The Contacts tool can insert a name, number or address once you allow it. Contacts stay on the phone, in memory only, and are never learned or sent. As a spell checker, LocalType reads the text other apps ask it to check and keeps none of it.</p>
