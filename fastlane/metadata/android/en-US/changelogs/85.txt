First release on F-Droid. It installs beside the LocalType download from dr.eamer.dev, and neither updates the other. Paste a copied image where the app accepts one, with location and camera details removed first. A switch under Your words and data hides LocalType from screenshots and screen sharing. With TalkBack you can type by lifting your finger. Blocking a typo brings its correction back, and Learned words can forget one word.
