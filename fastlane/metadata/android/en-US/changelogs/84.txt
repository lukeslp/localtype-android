First release on F-Droid. This build has its own package name, so it installs beside the LocalType download from dr.eamer.dev instead of replacing it, and neither updates the other. A switch under Settings, Your words and data, hides LocalType from screenshots, screen recordings and screen sharing; it is off until you turn it on. With TalkBack you can type by lifting your finger, you hear when the keys change, and more symbols have names, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
